StockLens

Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

StockLens is an inventory, catalog sharing and stock-management app used internally by garment-manufacturers, wholesalers and retail businesses to track their products, stock, and sales. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who to contact with questions. It applies to every business using StockLens and their employees.

1

Information We Collect

StockLens collects only what's needed to run a business's inventory operations:

  • Business & inventory data — product details (IDs, categories, sizes, colors, prices), stock transaction records (purchases, sales, quantities, dates, optional notes), and product photos, all entered by the business's own employees.
  • Account credentials — each business is provisioned with its own sign-in, managed through our backend provider (Supabase). We never see or store passwords in plain text.
  • Employee name — employees sharing a business's login enter their own first name once, used only to attribute stock entries for that business's own internal accountability. This is never visible to any other business.
  • Optional Google Drive access — if a business turns on cloud backup, the app requests Google's restricted drive.file scope, which only lets it see and manage backup files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in that Google account's Drive.
2

What We Don't Collect

StockLens has no advertising SDKs, no analytics or tracking services, and no location tracking. We never sell data to anyone, for any reason.
3

How Information Is Used

Information is used strictly to operate the app for the business that owns it: storing and displaying that business's catalog and stock records, generating reports and exports the business itself initiates (PDF or spreadsheet), and powering optional cloud backup. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside running the app.

4

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored with Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based backend provider. Each business's data is isolated from every other business at the database level, so one business can never see another's records. On the device itself, an optional PIN or biometric app-lock adds a further layer of protection before the app's contents are shown.

5

Data Sharing

We do not share or sell information to third parties for advertising or marketing. Data is only processed by the infrastructure needed to run the app: Supabase for backend storage, and Google, solely if a business enables Drive backup.

6

Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained for as long as a business's account stays active. Any business may request an export or full deletion of its data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

7

Children's Privacy

StockLens is a business tool intended for use by adult employees of registered businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated to reflect the most recent revision.
